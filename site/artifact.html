Price Elasticity of Supply

  • PES measures how much quantity supplied responds to a price change: %ΔQₛ / %ΔP.
  • Worked example: price $25→$30, supply 20k→50k → PES = 7.5 (highly elastic).
  • Five categories, from perfectly inelastic to perfectly elastic.

Drop in worksheets, slides, PDFs or a photo of the board. Evie reads them, groups what belongs together, and writes a proper note per topic.

Snap a photo of your timetable. Evie reads it and nudges you five minutes before each period, so you never miss the class you meant to record.
